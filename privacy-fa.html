What we collect

Your name and email; optionally phone, study level, support interest and message; along with submission time and a request ID. Please do not enter sensitive identification or health information.

Where it goes

Enquiries are stored in a private Google Sheet, the team receives an email notification and you receive an acknowledgement. Booking takes place through Cal.com; Google and Cal.com process relevant information under their policies.

Purpose & retention

Information is used to handle your enquiry and arrange appropriate support—not to subscribe you to marketing. Records are retained as needed for your enquiry or agreed services, subject to applicable obligations.

Your choices

Email vellumhouse.edu@gmail.com to request access, corrections or deletion, subject to applicable legal duties.

Minors

A parent or guardian should submit the form on behalf of a student under 18.
