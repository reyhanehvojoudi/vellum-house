The house

A house built around the student.

Vellum House brings continuity, thoughtful coordination and personal attention to academic life.

OUR PURPOSE

Education deserves a longer view.

Students need room to develop interests, change direction and build confidence. A good academic plan creates that room while keeping important decisions in view.

Vellum House was conceived as a continuous structure behind that journey: bringing specialists, priorities and milestones into conversation with one another.

WHAT GUIDES OUR WORK

Thoughtful by design.

01

Individuality

Decisions begin with this student's goals and circumstances, not an off-the-shelf route.

02

Continuity

Today's work connects with the next stage, and the plan is reviewed as needs evolve.

03

Ownership

Guidance strengthens the student's thinking and voice; it does not replace them.
