The house

A house built around the student.

Vellum House brings continuity, thoughtful coordination and personal attention to academic life.

Education deserves a longer view.

Students need room to develop interests, change direction and build confidence. A good academic plan creates that room while keeping important decisions in view. Vellum House was conceived as a continuous structure behind that journey: bringing specialists, priorities and milestones into conversation with one another.

Academic direction

Vellum House is founded by Reyhaneh Vojoudi, a physics educator and researcher with an MSc in Gravity and Cosmology. Her work across teaching, research and academic mentoring informs an approach that connects immediate learning needs with longer-term goals.
