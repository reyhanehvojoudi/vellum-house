Services

Expertise, with a shared direction.

Academic planning, teaching and mentoring coordinated around one student. The scope is agreed after a consultation.

01

Academic strategy

An individual roadmap connecting current priorities, curriculum decisions and longer-term ambitions.

02

Specialist instruction

One-to-one online teaching, selected and coordinated around the student's needs and wider academic plan.

03

Ongoing oversight

Regular evaluation, coordinated priorities and monthly reporting so the next step is always clear.

04

Curriculum & examinations

Guidance on IGCSE, A Level, IB, SAT and AP pathways, with choices informed by subject strengths and future requirements.

05

Profile & portfolio

Purposeful planning for research, competitions, projects and leadership, grounded in genuine interests.

06

University applications

University selection, timelines, essay feedback and interview preparation that preserve the student's own work and voice.

07

University & graduate support

Coursework planning, research direction, postgraduate applications and mentoring through the next stage of academic life.

08

Languages & future skills

Language learning, study habits and relevant skills integrated into an achievable plan for further study or career development.

BUILT FOR THE INDIVIDUAL

A considered scope, not a standard package.

The consultation is used to understand priorities and define the right combination of support.

Services and specialist availability are confirmed before an engagement begins.

Instruction is online; timing is coordinated with the student's location and schedule.

Fees, reporting arrangements and responsibilities are set out in the agreed proposal.

Admissions and scholarships are decided by institutions; no outcome is guaranteed.
