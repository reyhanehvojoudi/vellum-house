# Vellum House — 90-day client acquisition & conversion operating system

## Positioning

**Core promise:** Know the student deeply (Point A), choose an ambitious best-fit destination (Point B), and coordinate the teaching, mentorship, experiences and decisions between them. This is private academic management, not a tutoring marketplace or generic admissions package.

**Two-sentence English description:**
At Vellum House, we begin by understanding each student's talents, strengths, interests and opportunities, then define goals that bring out their potential. We design and manage the teaching, mentoring, projects, competitions and academic strategy that help them move from where they are to where they can go.

**دو جمله فارسی:**
در ولوم هاوس، ابتدا استعدادها، توانمندی‌ها، علایق و فرصت‌های هر دانش‌آموز را می‌شناسیم و هدف‌هایی متناسب با ظرفیت واقعی او انتخاب می‌کنیم. سپس آموزش، منتورینگ، پروژه‌ها، مسابقات و برنامه‌ریزی تحصیلی لازم را در مسیری اختصاصی طراحی، هماهنگ و پیگیری می‌کنیم.

## A simple service ladder

1. **Free 30-minute discovery conversation:** establish fit and goals, not a free fully designed plan.
2. **Personal assessment and roadmap:** paid, defined-scope diagnostic; clarify strengths, priorities, milestones and proposed support. Price after discovery.
3. **Full academic management:** recurring premium engagement with a lead mentor, teaching coordination, progress reviews and family reporting.
4. **Focused services:** specialist classes, competition preparation, assessment or university strategy sold individually with a defined scope.

Avoid discounts that train families to treat the flagship as ordinary hourly tutoring. Quote value and deliverables rather than only teaching hours. Define actual delivery capacity before increasing advertising spend.

## Funnel and operational ownership

**Traffic → Enquiry → Booked call → Attended call → Qualified proposal → Paying client → Retained client.**

The Google Sheet starts each lead as `New / Not booked`. It does **not** verify a Cal.com booking on its own. Assign the responsible person and record the following:

- First response sent, confirmation of booked appointment, meeting attendance.
- Parent / guardian involvement for under-18s, current study level, urgency, programme interests, student aspirations.
- Qualification: fit of needs with current service capacity, timescale, budget compatibility (discussed respectfully), decision-maker attendance and goals.
- Written follow-up: summary of needs, recommended next step, assessment or proposal scope, quote, start date and clear conditions.
- Outcome: Won, Lost, Not now, or Follow-up needed. Ask permission before any marketing communications.

## Suggested first 90 days

**Days 1–15: Reliability and credibility.** Complete Apps Script deployment, run real email and booking tests in EN/FA, secure student information, check mobile accessibility, set response ownership, and collect consent for genuine founder videos or case studies. No paid traffic until the funnel is confirmed.

**Days 16–45: Focused acquisition.** Use one clear CTA from the founder's established content channels to the Vellum House free consultation form. Publish short, useful examples of student pathways and the distinctive Point A → B method. Answer parent questions in accessible language rather than leading with high-status admissions claims.

**Days 46–90: Refine and scale.** Review enquiry-to-booking, booking-to-attendance, attended-to-proposal, proposal-to-client and renewal rates weekly. Test one change at a time: hero copy, form length, CTA framing or consultation preparation. Increase spend only when service delivery, conversion and unit economics are healthy.

## Financial discipline

Track **monthly recurring revenue**, one-off revenue, effective educator cost, coordination hours, customer acquisition cost, gross margin, refund exposure, enquiry source and churn. A high enquiry count is not profit unless the service team can deliver efficiently and retain satisfied families.

Do not invent testimonials, promise admission or imply that every international opportunity is open to every student. Trust and measurable delivery are the basis for sustainable premium pricing.
