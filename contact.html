BEFORE WE SPEAK

A few useful answers.

Who is Vellum House for?

School students, university applicants, undergraduates and graduate students seeking coordinated academic planning and ongoing support.

Can I ask about one subject?

Yes. Share the need in your enquiry. We can discuss whether focused online instruction or a broader plan is appropriate, subject to availability.

Are lessons online?

Yes. Teaching is online. Scheduling and any other arrangements are agreed before support begins.

Do you guarantee admission or scholarships?

No. Decisions rest with the institutions. Our role is to support informed choices and well-prepared, authentic applications.

Will parents receive updates?

The brochure describes monthly progress reporting. The content, recipients and arrangements should be agreed at the outset; university students' preferences and privacy also matter.

Is the consultation free?

No fee is assumed on this website. Any consultation charge and cancellation terms should be confirmed before the appointment is booked.
