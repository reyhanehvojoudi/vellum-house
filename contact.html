GOOD TO KNOW

A few helpful answers

Is the first call really free?+

Yes. The first 30-minute online conversation is free. Any assessment or ongoing service is proposed and priced separately.

Can we book only classes or mentoring?+

Yes. We can manage the complete journey or provide individual services.

How much do services cost?+

Fees are personalised after we understand your needs and agree the scope.

Can you guarantee university admission?+

No. Admissions and scholarship decisions belong to universities; we provide agreed planning and support, not guarantees.
