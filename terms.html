An overview of this website and the way a potential engagement begins.

Before we begin

01

A free first conversation

The initial consultation is free. Sending a form does not confirm a booking, admission to a programme or a paid engagement.

02

Beginning an engagement

An enquiry does not confirm an appointment or create a paid engagement. Services, availability, fees and responsibilities must be agreed separately.

03

Academic integrity

Students remain responsible for their own work. Planning, feedback and mentoring should support original thinking and authentic applications.

04

Outcomes

No admission, scholarship, examination result or career outcome is guaranteed. Individual progress depends on many factors, including the student's work and institutional decisions.

05

Agreeing the details

Before paid work begins, we agree the services, educators, schedule, fees, payment and cancellation terms in writing.
