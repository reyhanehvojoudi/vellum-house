How this website handles information and what happens when you choose to contact us.

Enquiry privacy notice

01

What we collect

Your name, grade or study level, city, phone number, email and enquiry message, plus the submission time, language and a request reference. Please do not submit identity documents, payment details or confidential health information.

02

Where your enquiry goes

Enquiries are stored in a private Google Sheet and emailed to reyhaneh.vojoudi@gmail.com and kasra01@gmail.com. An acknowledgement is sent to your email. Google and the website host process technical data to provide these services.

03

How we use it

We use your details to review the enquiry, arrange the free consultation and discuss appropriate services. The form does not subscribe you to marketing. This site uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

04

Your choices

Email reyhaneh.vojoudi@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion. Enquiry records are kept while the enquiry or agreed support is active; contact us about removal when support ends. Parents or guardians should submit school-student enquiries.
