Privacy

Your information, handled thoughtfully.

How this website handles information and what happens when you choose to contact us.

Operational details still to be completed: responsible business contact, applicable jurisdiction, retention periods and final service policies. These pages describe the current website and are not a substitute for an agreed service contract.

Information entered on this website

The enquiry form prepares a text summary in your browser. This version does not submit it to a server, save it in browser storage or use it for analytics. Clearing or leaving the page may remove the entered information.

When you choose to contact us

The consultation link opens the founder's existing website. Information you submit there is handled by that service's own form and processes. Review its information before sending personal details. Contact and retention details for Vellum House will be added here.

Hosting and external services

Fonts and website assets are hosted with the site. This version has no embedded analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies. The hosting provider may process technical request information as part of delivering the site.

Students and personal information

Please share only what is necessary for an initial conversation. Do not include identity documents, medical records, payment details or confidential school reports in the initial enquiry.

Questions or requests

Use the consultation contact route for privacy questions. The business contact responsible for privacy requests and the final retention policy must be added before operating a separate enquiry service.
