YOUR PRIVACY

Your information, treated with care.

An overview of what happens when you submit an enquiry and how you can exercise your choices.

VH.

What we collect

The contact person’s name and email; optionally phone, study level, area of interest and message; plus submission time and a request ID. Do not send sensitive identification, health or confidential documents using this form.

Where it goes

Enquiries are stored in a private Google Sheet and a notification is emailed to vellumhouse.edu@gmail.com. You receive an acknowledgement. Google and Cal.com process relevant data under their respective policies.

How we use it

To respond to your enquiry, enable booking, assess fit and discuss appropriate services. Submitting the form does not opt you into marketing.

Your choices

Email vellumhouse.edu@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion. We retain information only as needed to respond to enquiries or manage agreed services, subject to applicable obligations, and you may request deletion.

Minors

A parent or guardian should submit an enquiry for a student under 18. We request only what is useful at first contact.
